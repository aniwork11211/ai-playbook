# How to build your own AI assistant with Hermes

It works 24/7 while you are sleeping.

Use this file when the work is standing up that assistant. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md`. Do the steps in order. Tick a step only when it actually runs.

**Next:** Get a VPS

- [ ] **01 Get a VPS.** Go to Hostinger, get a VPS. That machine is the assistant's always-on computer.
- [ ] **02 Install Hermes.** Deploy Hermes Agent on the VPS.
- [ ] **03 Connect the AI brain.** Add your model: Claude, GPT, or Gemini.
- [ ] **04 Give Hermes memory.** Add your business, files, instructions, preferences, and knowledge.
- [ ] **05 Give it tools.** Enable Web Search, Browser, Terminal, Files, and Skills. Then it can do things.
- [ ] **06 Connect Telegram.** Message the assistant from your phone.
- [ ] **07 Give it your job.** Tell Hermes exactly what to handle: Research, Leads, Emails, Reports, Competitor Tracking, Daily Tasks.
- [ ] **08 Connect your apps.** Gmail, Calendar, Google Drive, CRM, GitHub, n8n.
- [ ] **09 Automate it.** Turn repetitive tasks into skills and workflows so you do not ask every time. Hermes does the work.
- [ ] **10 Make it proactive.** Do not build an AI that waits for you.
