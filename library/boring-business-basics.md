# Ten boring things that make starting a business easier

Source: hylden.b. Ten steps. No extra explanation is printed.

Use this file when the work is the early operating habit of a new business. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use only the step the task needs. The longer physical-product path stays in `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\library\product-business-steps.md`.

1. Write down who your customer is.
2. Ask customers what frustrates them.
3. Explain your offer in one sentence.
4. Calculate what each sale actually costs.
5. Keep your receipts organised.
6. Record the promises you make customers.
7. Follow up when you say you will.
8. Test demand before ordering large quantities.
9. Leave extra time for supplier delays.
10. Review what worked after each project.
