# Before you share the price

The price only makes sense once the buyer sees what it is worth to them. Naming it first is how leads go quiet.

Use this file when the work is a sales conversation and the price is about to come out. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and follow the steps in order. A sales message uses the sales rules file. Why someone says yes uses the psychology of selling file.

## 1. Dig into the challenge

Do not stop at the surface.

- “What challenge are you currently dealing with in your business?”
- “How is that showing up day to day?”
- “Where does solving this sit on your list right now?”
- “What happens if nothing changes in the next 6 months?”
- “When would you ideally want this solved?”
- “Have you set aside an investment for solving this?”

## 2. Get clear on the result they want

“If we were talking 6–12 months from now, what would need to have happened for you to feel amazing about it?”

## 3. Tie the offer to that result

Do not list features. Show how each part of the offer gets them to the specific outcome they just described.

## 4. Talk through the cost of doing nothing

This is the step that moves the buyer to act.

- “How long has this been going on?”
- “What has it cost you so far, in time, money, or energy?”
- “What does staying where you are mean for your goals this year?”

Leave the silence. Let them think it through.

## 5. Recap it in their words

Summarize their challenge, the result they want, and what is at stake, using the exact words they used. Then ask: “Did I get that right?”

Wait for the yes.

Then share the price. They are comparing it to the change they want and to what staying stuck is already costing them.
