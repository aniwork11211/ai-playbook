Use this file when the work is a Reel, a talking-head video, or this content system. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use only the tool or the step the task needs. Editing repos stay in the AI video editing repos file.

## Tools

1. **D-ID** — a Reel with a talking head, from text, without filming. The site on the slide is for AI video and interactive avatars.
2. **ZSKY AI** — video and image generation. The slide says without limits, 24/7.
3. **Pictory** — a Reel with a talking head, from text, without filming. The screen on the slide also shows text to video, URL to video, an AI video editor, summarize video, audio to video, PPT to video, and record.
4. **InVideo AI** — footage, subtitles, and narration from a text prompt. The slide says one workspace and 250+ creative models.
