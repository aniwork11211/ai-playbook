# 10 AI video editing repos

Source: Syntaix AI. Open-source tools for creators.

Use this file when the work is picking a repo to edit, clip, caption, or generate video. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use only the repo the task needs. Motion-design skills and renderers stay in the motion design repos file.

1. **OpenChatCut** — an open-source editor where you describe the edit in chat. Cut, reorder, add, or adjust clips, transitions, captions, effects, and audio. The timeline stays editable. `github.com/0xsline/OpenChatCut`
2. **FireRed-OpenStoryline** — describe the edit in everyday language. Cut and rearrange footage, and remove filler words and repeated sentences. Example: “Remove the filler words and cut the repeated sentence.” `github.com/FireRedTeam/FireRed-OpenStoryline`
3. **AutoClip** — turn a long podcast, interview, or lesson into short clips. It finds highlights, then can make subtitles, covers, and publishing copy. Long video, then AI highlights, then short clips. `github.com/zhouxiaoka/autoclip`
4. **Herk Edit Kit** — video-editing skills for coding assistants such as Codex and Claude Code. Cuts dead air, finds mistakes, organizes clips, and helps with captions, B-roll, and motion graphics. Coding assistant, then editing skills, then a faster workflow. `github.com/nateherkai/herk-edit-kit`
5. **AutoSubs** — generates subtitles on your machine, identifies speakers with timestamps, and sends captions into editing software. The path on the slide is video audio, then subtitles, then DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, or After Effects. `github.com/tmoroney/auto-subs`
6. **StoryToolkitAI** — search footage by meaning, collect the useful sections, and assemble a story timeline for the editing software. Search footage, find moments, assemble a story. `github.com/octimot/StoryToolkitAI`
7. **FunClip** — finds highlights in a long video and exports short vertical clips with captions for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok. Import the long video, the AI finds highlights, export the clips. `github.com/mcba1n/FunClip`
8. **NarratoAI** — turns a script or an idea into a video with AI voices, stock visuals, and subtitles. Voices and templates cover YouTube, Reels, and Shorts. Write a script, generate with AI, get a complete video. `github.com/edluffy/NarratoAI`
9. **BackgroundRemover** — removes an image background in one step and keeps the subject. Upload the image, remove the background, download the result. `github.com/nadermx/backgroundremover`
10. **LatentSync** — makes a face video match new audio, with lip movement from a voice, a script, or more than one language. Upload the video, add the audio, generate the lip sync, download. `github.com/bytedance/LatentSync`
