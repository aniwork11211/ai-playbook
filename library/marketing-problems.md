# Marketing problems

Five problems that can block an offer. Use this when the work is marketing and you need to find which problem is actually blocking the offer. Check these five before more ads or more content.

**Next:** Awareness

## Awareness

People cannot buy from a business they do not know exists. Show up where the target audience already spends time, on channels they actually watch, and make the brand easier to discover.

## Positioning

The brand sounds like everyone else, so there is nothing to remember. Find one clear angle and repeat that difference in content, messaging, and offers.

## Value proposition

People do not understand why the offer matters. Say who it is for, what problem it solves, and why they should care, in simple language.

## Targeting

The message is aimed at the wrong people, or at everyone. Define the ideal audience and their problems, needs, and goals, then speak only to them.

## Timing

Demand or market conditions are not ready. Too early is as hard as too late. Validate what customers need now, listen to feedback, and adapt the offer.

## Questions to ask first

- Can people discover you?
- Are you reaching the right people?
- Do they understand why the offer matters?
- Do they know why they should choose you?
- Does the market want this right now?
