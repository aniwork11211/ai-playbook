# Sites for UI and UX challenges

Practice briefs, not a gallery of finished interfaces. Places to study real screens stay in the vibecoder design sites file.

Use this file when the work is picking a challenge to design against. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use only the site the task needs.

The cover says six sites. Five were in the set.

1. **UX Tools** — real-world exercises, skills and tools, and work you can put in a portfolio. For beginners and for people who already design. `uxtools.co/challenges`
2. **Daily UI** — one design challenge a day for 100 days, plus surprise rewards. `dailyui.co`
3. **UI Coach** — project ideas, color palettes, font pairings, and references from award-winning and nominated sites. `uicoach.io`
4. **Fake Clients** — generated client briefs for practice. The slide shows a logo-design brief generator, plus brief generators, written briefs, a daily brief, and feedback. The paid tier on the slide is longer briefs, 200+ written briefs, and more feedback. `fakeclients.com`
5. **UX Challenge** — UX problems tied to products that exist, meant to become case studies. Examples on the slide: finding jeans that fit, pets home alone, and a transit alarm. `uxchallenge.co`
