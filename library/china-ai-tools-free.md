# Free Chinese AI tools

Source: @airesearches. Eleven tools. The cover says they are 100% free. Where a slide names the free tier, that is written under the tool. StepFun, Dreamina, GLM, and Kimi do not say free on their own slides.

Use this file when the work is picking one of these tools. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use only the tool the task needs. Running a DeepSeek or Kimi model locally through Colibri stays in `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\library\colibri.md`.

1. **Qwen Studio.** Alibaba. Write, research, code, analyze files, generate images, and run complex tasks across Qwen models, including Qwen 3.8 Max. The slide says all of that is free.
2. **StepFun 3.5.** Coding and AI agents. A 256K context window, fast, and built for multi-step tasks. Open source, and it can run locally on powerful hardware. The slide prints www.stepfun.ai.
3. **Dreamina.** ByteDance, for visual work. Generate images and videos, edit scenes, turn an image into video, and make visuals from a text prompt. The screen also shows AI Audio, AI Image, AI Agent, AI Video, and AI Avatar.
4. **GLM 5.3 Flash.** Zhipu AI. Multimodal: coding, reasoning, AI agents, and image understanding. 320 billion parameters, and it activates 18 billion per task. The slide says that is what makes it faster and cheaper to run.
5. **Kimi.** Moonshot AI. Long documents, research, and large amounts of information. It also makes presentations, websites, and reports, and takes on tasks that would otherwise need several tools.
6. **Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Pro.** Omnimodal: text, images, audio, and video. Built for complex projects, software engineering, and agents that run a long task. The slide says it is free on Xiaomi MiMo Studio.
7. **Ling 3.1 Flash.** InclusionAI, backed by Ant Group. Mixture-of-experts: 560 billion parameters, 25 billion active per token. The card on the slide says 262K context, price Free, released 2 October 2026, and shows OpenRouter. The text says it is free on OpenCode.
8. **DeepSeek.** The slide calls it a fast-growing alternative to ChatGPT and Claude. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash combines reasoning, coding, and tool use. The official chat stays free.
9. **ERNIE (Wenxin).** Baidu. Free on the web and in the app since April 2025. ERNIE 4.5 and X1, long-document analysis, advanced search, and image generation.
10. **Doubao.** ByteDance. Chat, write, translate, code, and generate images. Seedance 2.0 is built in for video, with free access after signing in.
11. **Yuanbao.** Tencent, on Hunyuan models. Chat, search the web including WeChat articles, analyze documents, and generate images. Web, mobile, and desktop.
