# 8 websites to start an online business

Use this file when the work is picking a site to start or run an online business. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use the one site whose job matches the work.

- Printify — create and sell custom products. Print on demand for the business.
- Voices.com — find voice opportunities worldwide. Voice talent for brands, companies, and creators.
- Patreon — build a membership community. Get paid for the content you create.
- Whatnot — live shopping for collectors and creators. Buy and sell unique items in real time.
- CPAlead — monetize traffic. Promote top offers and get paid for actions.
- Design Bundles — premium design assets for creators. Graphics, templates, fonts, and more.
- Podia — sell courses, memberships, and more. Share what you know.
- Flippa — buy and sell online businesses. Websites, domains, and digital assets.
