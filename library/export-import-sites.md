## Government and regulatory portals

1. **DGFT (Directorate General of Foreign Trade).** IEC registration, export and import policies, incentives, and trade notifications. www.dgft.gov.in
2. **ICEGATE (Indian Customs Electronic Gateway).** Customs clearance, filing shipping bills, bill of entry, and duty payment. www.icegate.gov.in
3. **ICEGATE e-Sanchit.** Paperless document submission for customs. www.icegate.gov.in/eSanchit.html
4. **Department of Commerce.** Trade policy, export and import statistics, and circulars. www.commerce.gov.in
5. **GST portal.** GST registration, return filing, LUT submission, and refunds. www.gst.gov.in
6. **PIB (Press Information Bureau).** Government trade notifications and policy updates. www.pib.gov.in

## Export promotion and support agencies

7. **FIEO.** The slide spells the expansion “Fedration of Indian Export Organisations.” Export guidance, trade fairs, and global buyer sellerr meets. www.fieo.org
8. **EPCs (Export Promotion Councils).** The slide prints the GST line: GST registration, return filling, LUT submission, and refunds. www.commerce.gov.in/tradepromotion/exportpromotion-councils/
9. **Exim Bank of India.** The slide prints the PIB line: government trade notifications and policy updates. https://www.eximbankindia.in
10. **APEDA.** Registration and export assistance for agri-food exporters. www.apeda.gov.in
11. **MPEDA.** The slide prints the GST line again: GST registration, return filing, LUT submission, and refunds. www.mpeda.gov.in
12. **Spices Board India.** Registration, data, and quality standards for spice exporters. www.indianspices.com
13. **Make in India.** Investment promotion and export-driven manufacturing support. www.makeinindia.com

## International trade and data

14. **India Trade Portal.** Tariffs, import regulations, and export opportunities by country. www.indiantradeportal.in
15. **WTO (World Trade Organization).** Global trade rules, agreements, and dispute resolution. www.wto.org
16. **ITC (International Trade Centre).** Market analysis, trade tools, and export potential data. www.intracen.org
17. **Trade Map (by ITC).** Analyze trade flows, demand trends, and export competitiveness. The slide prints www.trademao.org
18. **UN Comtrade database.** Global import and export data by product and country. www.comtradeplus.un.org
19. **Export Genius.** Indian and global shipment data, trade analytics, and buyer info. www.exportgenius.in
