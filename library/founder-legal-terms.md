# 40 legal terms every founder must know

Source: @startupbydoc. Short definitions as printed on the slides. For an Indian founder, before building, hiring, or raising.

Use this file when a legal or compliance word shows up. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use the one term the task needs. These lines are the poster’s definitions, not a legal opinion.

## Company and ownership

- Private Limited — privately owned company
- LLP — limited liability partnership
- Incorporation — company registration
- CIN — company identification number
- MOA — company’s permitted activities
- AOA — company’s internal rules
- Share Capital — capital represented by shares
- Equity — company ownership
- Shareholding — shares owned
- Limited Liability — limited personal financial risk

## Contracts and protection

- NDA — confidentiality agreement
- IPR — intellectual property rights
- Trademark — brand identity protection
- Copyright — original work protection
- Patent — invention protection
- Indemnity — compensation for specified losses
- Arbitration — private dispute resolution
- Force Majeure — extraordinary event clause
- Non-Compete — restriction on competing
- Founders’ Agreement — co-founder rights and duties

## Tax and compliance

- GST — goods and services tax
- GSTIN — GST identification number
- TDS — tax deducted at source
- PAN — permanent account number
- TAN — tax deduction and collection account number. The slide cuts off at “ac no”.
- ROC — Registrar of Companies
- DIN — director identification number
- DSC — digital signature certificate
- Annual Filing — mandatory periodic filings
- Statutory Audit — legally required audit

## Funding and investors

- Term Sheet — proposed investment terms
- Valuation — company’s estimated worth
- Dilution — reduced ownership percentage
- SHA — shareholders’ agreement
- SSA — share subscription agreement
- ESOP — employee stock options
- Vesting — earning equity over time
- Liquidation — priority in exit payouts
- Drag-Along Rights — required participation in a sale
- Tag-Along Rights — right to join a sale
