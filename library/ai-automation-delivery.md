## Two ways to deliver

Show both options together. A choice makes yes easier.

- One-time — full payment once. The system belongs to the client forever.
- Recurring — a small making fee plus monthly handling. We run and manage the system.

Who owns what:

- Server — one-time is the client’s. Recurring is ours.
- WhatsApp / Meta account — always the client’s.
- WhatsApp and calling usage — the client pays the provider directly, in both models.

These lines are service fees only.

## Server: Hostinger

One-time: the client’s own account. Recurring: our server.

- VPS, KVM 2 — about ₹12,000 a year. 2 CPU, 8 GB RAM. Use it for heavy automation: n8n, AI agents, calling agents, databases. Dedicated, not shared. Hostinger prices run on offers. Renewal costs more than the first term. The example on the slide is ₹799 a month for the offer and ₹1,199 a month on renewal. Tell the client that before they buy.
- Shared hosting — ₹250 to ₹300 a month. Use it for n8n, websites, landing pages, and lighter setups.

## WhatsApp agent setup

Connect the client’s Facebook / Meta Business account and WhatsApp number for them.

1. Get or create the client’s Meta Business account (Facebook ID).
2. Connect the business WhatsApp number to the API.
3. Link it to a platform such as AiSensy, Wati, or Interakt.
4. Get business verification and the display name approved.

The platform bill starts at about ₹2,000 a month. The client pays that bill directly.

## Meta message rates

On top of the platform fee, Meta charges for every message. India rates on the slide:

- Marketing, offers, promos, broadcasts — ₹0.86
- Utility, order updates, reminders — ₹0.115
- Authentication, OTP — ₹0.115
- Service, customer messages you reply to — first 1,000 a month free, then ₹0.115

₹0.115 is about 11.5 paise. Add 18% GST. Meta changes these often. Check Meta’s pricing page before quoting a client.

## Build, test, and deliver

Nothing goes live without three rounds of testing. Building the automation is about one week.

1. Build the automation.
2. We test it first.
3. The client’s team tests it.
4. Live testing with real leads.
5. Delivery and handover.
6. One month of support. If an issue comes up, fix it.

Get these from the client before the build:

- Meta Business admin access
- An AI API key, OpenAI or Gemini, on the client’s card
- Google Sheet or CRM access, where the leads will go
- Server login, one-time model only

## Recurring delivery

We build the system and manage it every month.

- Making charge — ₹15,000, once
- Handling charge — ₹10,000 a month, with 3 months paid together in advance
- Server — ours. We host it. n8n is included.
- Running cost — WhatsApp bills plus calling at ₹3 to ₹5 a minute, paid by the client as they use it

First payment: ₹15,000 + (₹10,000 × 3) = ₹45,000.

Handling covers monitoring, bug fixes, small changes and updates, and a monthly report of leads, messages sent, and calls made.

## One-time and recurring, side by side

| | One-time | Recurring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upfront | ₹60–70k+ | ₹45k (₹15k + 3 months) |
| Every month | Only the running cost | ₹10k plus the running cost |
| Server | Client’s | Ours |
| Maintenance | Client’s job, or an extra charge | Our job |
| Best for | Clients with their own tech team | Clients who want zero hassle |
| Our fees, year 1 | ₹60–70k+ | ₹1,35,000 |

Recurring is fixed income every month and a long-term client.

## Handover checklist

A clean handover is what brings repeat work and referrals.

1. Meta account and API keys in the client’s name. In the one-time model, the server too.
2. Scope in writing: what is included, and what is not.
3. A demo video and a simple guide on how to use it.
4. Share logins safely. Never over chat.
5. One month of support after delivery. Fix any issue.
6. The client’s final sign-off, then the last payment.

Do not create the client’s Meta account or API keys in your own name. If the client leaves later, both sides get stuck.
