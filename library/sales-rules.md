# Sales rules

Use this file when the work is a sales message, an offer, or a call to action. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md`.

Order: Understand the problem, connect with the customer, show the value, provide proof, make the next step clear.

**Next:** Problem first

## Problem first

People buy a solution to a problem they want solved. Pattern: Problem, then solution, then sale.

- Weak: "We sell accounting software."
- Strong: "We help business owners reduce the stress of managing taxes and finances."

## Outcome over features

Features say what it does. Outcomes say why it matters. Pattern: Feature, then benefit, then outcome.

- Weak: "5,000 mAh battery."
- Strong: "Charge it once and stay powered for up to two days."

## Speak their language

Use the customer's words. Their words beat your jargon.

- Weak: "High-converting sales funnel."
- Strong: "Get more customers without constantly running ads."

## Emotion sells

Emotion can move the decision. Logic helps them justify it. Emotion first, then logic.

- Weak: "This course includes 20 modules."
- Strong: "Build the confidence and skills to approach sales with clarity."

## Proof over promises

Trust comes from evidence. Show results, testimonials, case studies, or measured outcomes. Claim plus proof equals belief.

- Weak: "We're the best marketing agency."
- Strong: "We helped 50 brands grow. Here are the results and case studies."

## Urgency > discounts

Give a genuine reason to act now instead of endless discounts.

## Clarity closes sales

Confusion creates hesitation. Say what you offer, who it is for, what result it delivers, and the next step. Pattern: What, who, result, next step.

- Weak: "DM for details."
- Strong: "DM START to learn how we can help you build a stronger sales system."
