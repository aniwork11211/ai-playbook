# Product business, ten steps

A physical-product path: idea, proof people will pay, then make, brand, price, site, operations, content, launch, and keep testing. The slides name Australia as the home market. No author was on the set.

Use this file when the work is one of these steps. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use only the step the task needs. Sales calls use the before-the-price file. Company and contract words use the founder legal terms file.

## 1. Find your big idea

Find a problem to solve. Look for pain in products that already exist. Feed reviews into Claude and ask for the complaints that repeat. Prompt: “What problems in [industry] are customers currently paying to solve badly?”

Look for gaps. Compare competitors with Claude. Traffic and audience: SimilarWeb. Market research: Exploding Topics. Competitor analysis, for example ShopHunter.

Name the advantage. Start from your interests and skills. Give Claude your background and ask: “What unfair advantages could I build a business around?”

Watch trends on social media. Use Perplexity to find products and categories growing overseas before they hit Australia. Ad-spy sites for TikTok trends: Minea and PiPiADS.

## 2. Validate the idea

Ask whether there is enough demand. Have Claude name the biggest assumptions, and argue why the business could fail. Prompt: “What would I need to prove before spending $10k on this idea?” Check Google Trends and keyword volume.

Research the customer in Reddit, TikTok comments, reviews, and forums. Feed those comments into Claude and group needs, objections, and frustrations. Survey the target market with Typeform.

Before building: a landing page, ads, and emails or pre-orders. The slide says likes are not validation, and that people paying is. The sentence is cut off there.

## 3. Create the product

Turn the idea into a brief. Use Claude for product specs, features, materials, and target cost. The slide cuts off at “target ost.” Prompt: “Turn this idea into a manufacturer-ready product brief.”

Find suppliers on Alibaba, Global Sources, and Made-in-China. Use Claude to compare quotes, MOQs, lead times, and terms.

Visualise before you produce. Mock-ups: Photoroom. Use Claude for supplier questions and a sample checklist.

## 4. Build the brand

Position it. Give Claude the customer, the competitors, and the idea, and ask for 10 positioning angles. Prompt: “Why should someone buy from me instead of [competitor]?” Then define the brand identity.

Visual identity. Pinterest for inspiration and a moodboard. Logos and branding: Canva AI and Looka. Use those tools for a brand bible: tone, colours, font, imagery, and dos and don’ts. The last visible word on the slide is “Logo.”

Define the tone of voice.

## 5. Do the numbers

Give Claude the costs, the price, and the assumptions. Calculate gross margin, breakeven, a CAC target, and the sales required.

Forecast in LivePlan, or cheaply in Google Sheets.

Pressure-test it. Prompt: “What assumptions in this forecast are unrealistic?” Run a best case, a base case, and a worst case. Work backwards: how many units have to sell each month for this to be worthwhile?

## 6. Build the website

Shopify, including Sidekick and its AI website builder, so this step does not need a developer.

Use Claude for the homepage, FAQs, About, and product descriptions. Prompt: “Rewrite this around customer benefits, not product features.”

Imagery. Backgrounds and product shots: Photoroom. Graphics and banners: Canva. Get it live. Done beats perfect.

## 7. Operations and admin

Register the business. Open a bank account. Trademark the name. Buy the domain.

Accounting: Xero. Payments: Stripe or Shopify Payments. Ship it yourself, or find a 3PL.

## 8. Create the content

Ask Claude for content pillars, hooks, and a 30-day calendar. Feed it viral competitor posts and ask what patterns they share.

Graphics: Canva. Video: CapCut or Descript. Turn one video into clips, captions, and posts.

UGC: ask friends, or find creators on Billo and JoinBrands.

## 9. Launch and market it

Ask Claude: “Build me a 30-day launch strategy with a $2k budget.”

Ads. Competitor research: Meta Ad Library. AI video ads: Creatify. Use Claude for 20 hooks, angles, and variations.

Email and automation: Klaviyo. Use AI for the welcome flow, the abandoned-cart flow, and the launch flow.

Test, learn, iterate.

## 10. Test, learn, iterate

Track it. Google Analytics. Ask Claude to read sales, traffic, conversion, and customer feedback.

Double down. Scale the products that sell. Put more budget on the ads and channels that win. Turn organic posts that perform into paid ads.

Keep testing price, offers, landing pages, creatives, and new products. A/B tests: VWO or Shopify apps.

Grow repeat purchase, and email or SMS. Add products, channels, and markets. Prompt: “What are my highest-impact growth opportunities?”

Launch is where the learning starts.
