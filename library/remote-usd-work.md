# Remote work paid in USD

Source: @thesanskaarsingh. Five places to look. The post’s point is to build the skill, a portfolio, and the habit of applying, and to get better at saying what you are worth to an international client.

Use this file when the work is finding remote roles paid in USD. Open it from `C:\Users\ANISH\Documents\ai-playbook\LIBRARY.md` and use only the board the task needs.

1. **Skipthedrive** — remote work from companies around the world, done from home.
2. **Built In** — higher-paying tech work at startups and large companies.
3. **Underdog.io** — a match with tech companies hiring software engineers and developers. The site on the slide is a closed network: the profile stays invisible, staffing agencies are out, and vetted hiring partners come to you.
4. **Jobgether** — remote work at companies that hire people from different countries. The screen on the slide is aimed at senior professionals.
5. **Wellfound (AngelList)** — apply directly to a startup, salaries in USD, no recruiter in the middle.
