## Airbyte

Open-source data integration platform. Engineers move data from hundreds of sources into a warehouse. Remote-first, with an open-source community behind it. The slide says 400+ employees and 13 open roles.

Hiring: engineering, account management, recruiting, sales, technical support.

## Linear

Issue tracking and project management for software teams that care about speed and design. The slide calls it the tool developers actually want to use. Remote-first, 150+ employees across North America and the U.K.

Hiring: production, human resources, customer experience, product management, engineering, design, marketing, sales.

## HubSpot

CRM, marketing, and sales platform, described as a go-to for growing businesses. One of the bigger companies in the set. The slide says they leaned into remote work hard since 2020, and that they have 7,000+ employees.

Hiring: customer success, business technology, sales, product and engineering, operations, marketing.

## Close

CRM for inside sales teams who live and die by the phone and email. Fully remote, described as tight-knit and fast at shipping. The slide says 100+ employees across the U.S. and seven open roles.

Hiring listed: data, engineering.

## GitLab

The slide is titled GitLab. The body printed under that title is: an API platform for authentication and enterprise features (SSO, Directory Sync, and admin portals) so B2B SaaS teams can be enterprise-ready without building from scratch. Fully distributed, with 80+ remote roles across the U.S., and global opportunities too.

Hiring: sales, marketing, product, engineering, security, administration.

## Pulumi

Infrastructure as code. Engineers use Python, TypeScript, and Go instead of config files to manage their cloud. Remote-first, 100+ employees.

Hiring: solutions architects, engineering, product, enterprise sales.
